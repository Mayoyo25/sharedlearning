How it works

From idea to image in about a minute

No design skills needed. The template handles layout, spacing and contrast. You bring the list.

Pick a template

Start from one of six layouts, or open a blank canvas. Switch template, colors and size whenever you like.

Edit on the canvas

Click text to change it, drag numbers to reorder, and add logos or emoji. Text shrinks to fit as your list grows.

Download and post

Export a PNG, JPG or PDF at twice the design size, or copy the image straight to your clipboard.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

A focused tool for one job: making list graphics that look good. It loads fast and works on phones.

Edit right on the canvas

Click any text and type. What you see is exactly what you export, with no form on the side to fill in.

Drag to reorder

Grab a number and move it. Add, duplicate or delete items with a hover toolbar or the keyboard.

Real brand logos

Search thousands of official brand logos, or match every item automatically. Upload your own images, too.

Pick-and-mix typefaces

49 open-source fonts. Style the whole graphic, or select just a few words to change their color and font.

Every platform, every size

Portrait, square, story, tall, wide and banner. Exported at twice the design size for sharp results.

Handles in the footer

Add up to four social handles with platform icons. Text scales down so they always stay on one line.

Annotate

Draw arrows, lines, boxes and circles to point at what matters. They export with the graphic.

Import anything

Paste a list, drop in JSON, or pipe it from an automation. JSON-RPC is understood for AI-tool integrations.

Private by design

Your lists and images stay in your browser and autosave on your device. No account, no tracking, no watermark.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no paid tier, no watermark and no sign-up. The code is open source under the MIT license, so you can use it, change it and host it yourself.

Does my work get uploaded anywhere?

No. Your lists, images and exports are created in your browser and autosaved in its local storage. The only network requests are for the libraries and fonts the page loads from public CDNs, and for brand-logo search, which sends the name you type to the Iconify service.

Why does it need an internet connection?

The editor loads its libraries (Sortable, html2canvas, jsPDF) and Google Fonts from CDNs. Logo search also calls the Iconify API. If you self-host and bundle those files, everything except logo search can work offline.

Can I use the graphics commercially?

The graphics you make are yours. The typefaces are open source (SIL OFL or Apache 2.0). Brand logos belong to their owners, so check each brand's usage guidelines before using them in paid or promotional work.

Which browsers work?

Current Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox, on desktop and phone. Editing is easiest on a larger screen, but the mobile layout works for quick edits and exports.

Can I connect it to my own tools?

Yes. The editor accepts JSON, including JSON-RPC 2.0 envelopes, and exposes window.ListGraphic.load() and .get() for scripts and automations. See the docs for the format.

How do I self-host it?

It is plain static HTML. Put index.html, docs.html and editor.html in any folder and serve them, for example with python3 -m http.server. There is no build step.
